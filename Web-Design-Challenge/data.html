Weather dataset

547 cities from the OpenWeatherMap API, collected on 13 June 2018. Search, filter by country, sort any column, and export what you see. Download the raw CSV.

Columns: temperatures are daily maximums in °F; humidity and cloudiness are percentages; wind speed is in mph; the date is when OpenWeatherMap recorded the observation (UTC).
