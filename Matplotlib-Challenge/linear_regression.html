Regression: Mouse Weight vs. Average Tumour Volume

Analysis

The regression shows how much the average tumour volume (dependent variable) changes as mouse weight (independent variable) changes.

The R² value is 0.70: mouse weight explains about 70% of the variation in average tumour volume around its mean. That is a fairly strong linear relationship — heavier mice tend to have larger average tumour volumes — though, as with any observational association, it does not on its own prove causation.

Key takeaway

Mouse weight is a strong predictor of average tumour volume (R² ≈ 0.70).
