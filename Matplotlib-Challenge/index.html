Which drug regimen shrinks tumours most?

An animal study of squamous cell carcinoma (SCC): mice received one of ten drug regimens, including a placebo, and tumour volume was tracked over 45 days. Cleaned, analysed and visualised with Pandas and Matplotlib.

Results

Key findings

  • Capomulin and Ramicane had the smallest final tumour volumes of the four regimens compared medians about 36–38 mm³, versus about 60 mm³ for Infubinol and Ceftamin. See the box plot.

  • For mice on Capomulin, body weight strongly predicts average tumour volume: R² ≈ 0.70, about +0.95 mm³ per gram. See the regression.

  • Capomulin has the most tumour measurements (230) and Propriva the fewest (about 160) regimens with more measurements kept more mice in the study for longer. See the counts.

  • The population is balanced by sex (124 female, 125 male), so sex is unlikely to bias the comparison. See the split.

Caveat baseline weight: mice on Capomulin and Ramicane were lighter at the start (≈20 g on average vs. 27–28 g for every other regimen). Because weight is strongly linked to tumour volume, part of their advantage may reflect body weight rather than the drug alone. A fairer test would adjust for weight or compare change from baseline.

How it was done

Methodology

  1. Clean

    Combined the study data in Pandas and removed a mouse with duplicated timepoint records, leaving 248 mice for the treatment analysis.

  2. Aggregate

    Summarised measurements by regimen and sex with Pandas groupby operations.

  3. Visualise

    Bar, pie and box plots in Matplotlib; outliers beyond 1.5×IQR shown as points.

  4. Model

    Linear regression of mouse weight on average tumour volume for Capomulin (R² ≈ 0.70).
